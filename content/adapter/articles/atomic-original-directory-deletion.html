Atomic original directory deletion

A backend that can commit a whole admitted subtree in one native transaction can offer atomic-original-directory-delete-v1, in addition to original-directory-admission-v1. This does not enable the separate individual child or final-directory effect capabilities. No default handshake enables it. DeleteOriginalDirectoryAtomically returns OriginalDirectoryDeletedAtomically; ordinary MutationComplete is insufficient.

The request includes the complete original admission request and backend admission, and binds their fingerprints to the Host's sealed manifest. All operations must be conditional Delete operations in the admitted leaf-first order. Move, upload and directory creation are outside this capability. A staged cross-instance move may use this operation for source retirement only after the Host has durably verified the complete destination copy and authorized every source operation. The capability itself supplies no copy or verification proof.

The backend revalidates its durable admission, complete inventory, original generations and conditions under the same qualified history and exclusion boundary as its native transaction. It reserves the immutable Host authorization before source mutation. The same admission or any of its operation IDs cannot be rebound to another plan, manifest or individual-effect execution. Capability negotiation, hashes and SDK shape checks cannot establish these native facts.

All original removals and one immutable receipt commit together. No child result is returned or counted as accepted before the batch commits. The receipt retains every operation ID, original address and original generation in the authorized order, as well as the request, admission and manifest fingerprints and backend commit evidence. It is a whole-batch receipt; it is not a chain of provisional AdapterOwnedChildEffectReceipt values. It has no fabricated deletion revision.

Before commit, cancellation or process death must preserve every original and leave no accepted receipt. After commit, a lost reply, cancellation or process restart must recover the identical receipt without opening or mutating a later occupant at the original path. The backend refuses changed authorization and missing, corrupt or incompatible retained evidence. Host acknowledgement follows durable acceptance of the full receipt and remains a separate product step.

AdapterAtomicDirectoryDeletionWire checks protocol, both capabilities, request/session/instance correlation, a 1 MiB body limit and exact reply membership. The dispatcher validates before invoking IAdapterAtomicDirectoryDeletionBackend and validates its reply. Signed Host/Worker integration must separately qualify durability, native history/exclusion, root authorization and official journal ACK. The SDK contract checks use a synthetic backend and do not claim those outcomes.
